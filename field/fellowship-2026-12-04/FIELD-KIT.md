## The smallest complete Fellowship

A guest can enter, be welcomed, participate in a simple shared gathering, find a clear way to help or receive help without pressure, and leave safely. It must work even if there is **no vendor, DJ, website, new AV equipment, donation collection, game, photography, or extra volunteer**. Food, extended music and distribution are included only when actual people and safe handling are confirmed.

The **minimum viable evening** is:
1. **Welcome:** an organizer greets guests and explains where to sit and how the evening flows.
2. **Gather:** shared conversation; meal or refreshments **only if** separately provided, approved and handled safely.
3. **Prayer/music:** a host-approved prayer segment and one reliable acoustic or existing-gear music set.
4. **Human connection:** neighbors can offer contact privately and voluntarily, or leave without registering.
5. **Close:** clear end time, cleanup, accountability for the space and a next-point-of-contact.

**The room being available is settled, not a question to ask again.** The remaining unknowns are practical ones: exact access times, pastor/prayer timing, Deb's food role, the pastor's son's performance, safety and food-handling responsibility, sound, accessibility, cleanup, communication approval, and whether any fundraising or distributions are actually authorized.
