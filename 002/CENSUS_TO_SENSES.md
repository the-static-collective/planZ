# PLANZ-002 — CENSUS → SENSES

## Mutation Engine

### One-line law

> **The census may sense pressure. It may not rewrite history or choose the future.**

PLANZ-001 answers:

> What futures did we open, and where are they now?

PLANZ-002 asks:

> What becomes perceptible once those futures are held together without collapsing them?

The move is:

```text
CENSUS
  ↓
SENSES
  ↓
ADDRESSABLE PRESSURE
  ↓
BOUNDED MUTATION SPACE
  ↓
CANDIDATE FUTURES
  ↓
SEPARATE ADMISSION
```

PLANZ-002 is not a global planner and not an autonomous roadmap generator.

It is a deterministic, provenance-preserving **mutation instrument** over the census.

---

# 1. Why 001 naturally becomes 002

A census is not merely a list.

Once every plan has:
- source provenance;
- present disposition;
- descendants;
- remaining edge;
- recovery mode;
- uncertainty;

the collection itself begins to expose structure.

Examples:

- two old plans may be carrying complementary missing organs;
- one stranded implementation may now sit beside the exact runtime that would make it cheap to recover;
- a law may have reincarnated repeatedly without its original interface;
- five separate plans may keep reopening the same gap;
- an abandoned project may contain one organ still absent everywhere else;
- a completed plan may now function as infrastructure for an unfinished one.

PLANZ-001 records those facts.

PLANZ-002 makes them **perceptible as bounded signals**.

```text
CENSUS != SENSES

CENSUS + DERIVED OBSERVATION
    = SENSE FIELD
```

The sense field remains derived testimony.

It does not modify the census record.

# 2. Iron Lung ancestry

PLANZ-002 directly borrows the constitutional shape of Iron Lung without making planZ the owner of Iron Lung.

Iron Lung's central questions:

```text
What crossed?
What is it continuous with?
By what authority may it move or change?
```

become, for one plan:

```text
SUBSTANCE
  What actually exists?
  source files, code, branches, tests, receipts, artifacts

LINEAGE
  What did this descend from?
  What descended from it?
  What law or organ survived elsewhere?

AUTHORITY
  What is current?
  What was admitted?
  What remains only proposal, branch, test, or memory?
```

No strand substitutes for another.

A brilliant old implementation with no current admission remains historically real and presently stranded.

A later descendant does not retroactively merge into its ancestor.

A strong candidate mutation does not acquire authority by surviving pressure.

## Addressable Absence

Iron Lung's **Addressable Absence** becomes one of PLANZ-002's foundational senses.

Absence is not represented as generic missingness.

It is bound to an attributable promise:

```text
promised artifact / gate / capability
        ↓
searched present field
        ↓
not found / unresolved / displaced
        ↓
ADDRESSABLE GAP
```

The gap keeps:
- plan identity;
- promised edge;
- search basis;
- current disposition;
- uncertainty;
- last observation cut.

This allows absence to participate in composition without pretending absence proves impossibility.

# 3. APERTURE → TRIAD → BRAID

PLANZ-002 may use Iron Lung's later semantic tensor as a reading discipline.

## APERTURE

- **Signal** — directly observed evidence in the census.
- **Context** — neighboring records and declared relations required to interpret the signal.
- **Gap** — an attributable missing, unresolved, conflicting, or not-yet-crossed edge.

## TRIAD

- **Fact** — census-grounded observation.
- **Idea** — candidate interpretation or mutation.
- **Relation** — explicit connection between records.

## BRAID

- **Substance**
- **Lineage**
- **Authority**

The engine does not need to materialize all 27 combinations.

The tensor is a refusal discipline:

> **Ambiguity is information. Do not resolve a future merely because the machine can generate one.**

# 4. SenseFrame v0

A `SenseFrame` is a deterministic read-model derived from one exact PlanRecord.

V0 recognizes six sense families.

## 4.1 GAP

Present when an explicit remaining edge exists.

Examples:
- promised artifact absent;
- declared acceptance gate unrun;
- implementation plan never started;
- live witness pending.

## 4.2 STRANDING

Present when meaningful implementation exists outside present owning authority.

Examples:
- tested code on an unmerged branch;
- implementation far behind current main;
- finished-ish organ never admitted.

## 4.3 LINEAGE

Present when the record has meaningful ancestor/descendant structure.

Examples:
- reincarnated capability;
- supersession;
- re-port;
- one organ surviving in another project.

## 4.4 AMBIGUITY

Present when the current state cannot be safely classified as one simple future.

Examples:
- UNKNOWN disposition;
- conflicting descendant evidence;
- low-confidence classification;
- apparent abandonment with unresolved non-Git possibility.

## 4.5 RECOVERABILITY

Present when the existing record already names a bounded recovery posture.

V0 reads the declared recovery mode rather than inventing one.

## 4.6 COMPOSITION PRESSURE

V0 does **not** infer arbitrary cross-plan compatibility.

Composition pressure may be emitted only from:
- an explicit PlanRecord relation;
- a user-declared pair;
- a later planZ-owned relation receipt.

```text
similarity != relation
co-occurrence != composition
shared vocabulary != shared authority
```

# 5. Lung / Heart split

This is the central PLANZ-002 boundary.

## LUNG

The Lung may:
- inspect SenseFrames;
- retain multiple candidate futures;
- recurse through bounded proposal-space;
- preserve gaps and residue;
- produce mutation candidates;
- produce pressure questions;
- refuse to produce a candidate.

The Lung may not:
- alter source PlanRecords;
- select a winning future;
- create project authority;
- merge branches;
- open implementation work in another repository by implication.

## HEART

The Heart is **not implemented inside the mutation engine**.

A Heart beat is a separately authorized present consequence:

```text
candidate mutation
      +
human / owning-project admission
      ↓
new actual history
```

The Heart may eventually be represented by an external receipt, PR, issue, explicit human decision, or project-local admission gate.

PLANZ-002 records the returned consequence.

It does not manufacture it.

> **The Lung may breathe through possibilities. The Heart beats only when consequence becomes present history.**

# 6. The Maxhinals

The Daily Slice Maxhinal contributes the executable mutation grammar.

The founding laws survive:

> **SAME MACHINE. DIFFERENT HANDS.**

> **THE MACHINE MAY BURN THE SLICES. IT MAY NOT BURN THE RECEIPTS.**

For PLANZ-002:

```text
PLAN RECORDS = FUEL
SENSE FRAMES = OBSERVED FIELD
MUTATION CHAMBERS = DECLARED OPERATIONS
CANDIDATES = DERIVED INTERMEDIATES
RIDE = APPEND-ONLY MUTATION RECEIPT
```

The plan records themselves are immutable historical gas.

Derived outputs never silently become PlanRecords.

## Maxhinal chambers v0

### REANIMATE

Input:
- one STRANDED or PARTIAL record;
- declared recovery mode RESURRECT.

Purpose:
- propose the smallest continuation from recoverable history.

It does not merge the fossil.

### RECONSTITUTE

Input:
- one materially diverged stranded implementation;
- recovery mode REPORT.

Purpose:
- propose a clean re-port onto present authority.

Old ancestry remains witness; current authority remains destination.

### GRAFT

Input:
- one REINCARNATED or recomposition-marked record;
- explicit surviving organ/law.

Purpose:
- carry only the useful organ into a new candidate composition.

It does not restore the ancestor wholesale.

### PROBE

Input:
- UNKNOWN / INVESTIGATE / ambiguous record.

Purpose:
- generate the smallest discriminating evidence request.

It prefers one answerable question over a speculative completion.

### PRESSURE

Input:
- any candidate mutation.

Purpose:
- ask what would falsify, block, or materially change the candidate.

Survival does not promote the candidate.

### CROSS

Input:
- exactly two PlanRecords;
- an explicit human/planZ-declared hinge.

Purpose:
- produce a bounded composed candidate preserving both ancestries and the declared hinge.

V0 refuses automatic pair discovery.

### PRUNE

Input:
- one record plus an explicit present refusal/retirement reason.

Purpose:
- make non-pursuit attributable.

PRUNE does not erase the plan.

It creates a candidate historical disposition update requiring separate admission.

# 7. Mutation ride

A PLANZ-002 mutation session is append-only.

Conceptual shape:

```json
{
  "schema": "static-collective/planz-mutation-ride/v0",
  "rideId": "...",
  "censusCut": "...",
  "inputs": [],
  "operations": [],
  "candidates": [],
  "residuals": [],
  "refusals": [],
  "authority": "none",
  "selection": "NONE"
}
```

Required laws:

```text
census input != mutation candidate
candidate != selected future
selected future != project admission
pressure survival != truth
derived relation != historical relation
ride receipt != authority
```

A ride may be replayed.

A changed census cut produces a descendant ride, not a rewrite.

# 8. First executable proof — SENSE-001

PLANZ-002 begins model-free.

No LLM is required to prove the constitutional boundary.

## Inputs

Use the existing seed PLANZ registry.

## Required proof

1. Parse valid PlanRecords.
2. Derive one deterministic SenseFrame per record.
3. Show GAP where a remaining edge exists.
4. Show STRANDING only when implementation evidence supports it.
5. Preserve UNKNOWN / ambiguity rather than filling it.
6. Generate one unary mutation candidate from each declared recovery posture.
7. Refuse CROSS without an explicit hinge.
8. Permit CROSS with an explicit hinge while preserving both plan ancestries.
9. Emit `authority: none` and `selection: NONE`.
10. Re-running identical input yields byte-equivalent semantic output.

## Non-goals

SENSE-001 does not:
- crawl GitHub;
- complete PLANZ-001;
- infer semantic similarity;
- use embeddings;
- invoke a model;
- rank a winner;
- mutate project repositories;
- create a universal ontology;
- decide that an old plan is desirable.

# 9. What makes 002 complete

PLANZ-002 is not complete when it generates lots of ideas.

It is complete when the following boundary is executable and witnessed:

```text
CENSUS
  ↓ exact read
SENSE FRAME
  ↓ deterministic derivation
MUTATION CANDIDATES
  ↓ bounded chambers
RESIDUE / REFUSAL / RECEIPT
  ↓
NO AUTOMATIC HEARTBEAT
```

Completion gate:

- [ ] SenseFrame schema is frozen.
- [ ] mutation-candidate schema is frozen.
- [ ] SENSE-001 deterministic engine exists.
- [ ] unary Maxhinal chambers are executable.
- [ ] CROSS requires an explicit hinge.
- [ ] immutable census input is tested.
- [ ] deterministic replay is tested.
- [ ] ambiguity/refusal are first-class.
- [ ] authority and selection remain none.
- [ ] one human-reviewed mutation ride is preserved.
- [ ] one candidate is deliberately **not** admitted, proving that generation does not force consequence.
- [ ] one separately admitted candidate returns as new history through a later explicit project/human edge.

The final item is the first real **Heart-return witness**.

Until then the Lung may be alive, but PLANZ-002 must not claim a heartbeat.
