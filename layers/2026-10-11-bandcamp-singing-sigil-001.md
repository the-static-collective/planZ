## Why Bandcamp belongs at this seam

The user supplied their exported Bandcamp lyric text, \`BandCamp (4).txt\`.
It contains a preexisting history of imagined sonic, electrical, spatial
and provenance ideas. Its themes are evidence about the *creative development
of the project*, not evidence that hidden modem packets, geophones or live
room sensing were actually deployed in the recorded tracks.

The first recomposed specimen names three source particulars:

| Proposed cue | Track | Export release | What it motivates |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| SENSE | The House Takes Attendance | The Autodiscography - The Road So Far | Voluntary noticing of a room/encounter |
| ENCOUNTER | Tesla in the Orchard | The Autodiscography - 183 | Controlled physical transduction question, not a measured effect |
| PRESERVE | # mundaneWORMHOLE | The Autodiscography - Creepy Disco | Distinct origin histories surviving a crossing |

These are **three tracks drawn from different releases**, deliberately assembled
for the new proposed score. Historical album membership is not rewritten.
The text export provides lyrics, titles and release-section context, **not**
validated audio bytes, exact track IDs, permissions for derivative publishing,
or an audible encoded cue.

Unlike a simple planning document, PR #89 contains source code, a checked-in

## Why the album-as-instrument idea is different

Bandcamp can remain an ordinary listening/publishing entryway. A separate
locally obtained sidecar file (where allowed by the owner/publisher) may declare
optional relationships between particular recordings and inspectable proposed
encounters. Absence of sidecar, decoder or network does not diminish playback.

The machine never treats the Spotify/Bandcamp/Suno-style title as a verified
recording or account permission. Sidecar identity, derivative WAV identity,
captured acoustic observation and human choice remain different particulars.

A real future acoustic cue must be authored into an **authorized derivative**
and decoded from measured audio, not magically found inside older recordings.
A rendered Green Grammar sigil keeps an independently scannable standard QR,
with ornament outside the quiet zone. Its visual form cannot create evidence
or authorization.

## Gates and failure conditions

- **F0 — software candidate:** exact-head native CI and hostile tests,
  replay, source mutation withdrawal, no executed effects. Only this software
  gate is being attempted in PR #89.
- **F1 — physical singing sigil:** verified source/derivative WAV permissions;
  standards-compliant printed QR; a deliberately encoded audible cue;
  **20 independently logged QR attempts and 20 acoustic trials**, failures
  and negative controls retained, separate evidence digests and uncertainty.
- **F2 — volunteered listening encounter:** opt-in local playback-state
  receipts for the three tracks, explicit stop/revoke, repeated/skipped/
  out-of-order events. Never infer playback from a playlist declaration.
- **F3 — human door:** Workbench/Abundent optional inspect/decline/admit UI,
  owner-local reLATTE crossing only under an independent capability.
  No score or carrier directly commands execution.

**Fail closed:** unknown origin, unsigned claims, ambiguous audio, wrong
referent, repeated cues, incomparable clocks, missing source, expired
participant consent or missing permissions must remain HOLD/REFUSED.
Coincident references do not prove two independently witnessed events.
