## 1. The missing top layer

MASTER-SERVANT handles *how* a physical/digital encounter is offered: paper/QR sigils, authorized recorded sonic cues, score sequences, read-only local processors, opted-in roomtone, and separately staged optical/RF/scrap experiments. The carrier is not the meaning.

TOPPER-001 proposes a **human-facing festival world** where different doors remain independently real:

- **Scripture:** inspect a verified Revival source-backed passage, token, or world address where an actually compiled corpus supports it, or view an unresolved plain-paper citation.
- **Worship:** locally hosted gathering, silence, prayer, testimony, choirs and wildly varied music under that host's consent and boundaries.
- **Gift:** a physical Giving Tree with *I NEED / I HAVE / I CAN MAKE / I WITNESSED*; private needs stay with a trusted human steward.
- **Music and story:** optionally offered artifact/recording/reading, with performer, source version, rights and separately scoped publication permission.
- **Return:** local churches and neighbors keep relationships and their own tree branch when the caravan leaves. Consented song, handmade object or proposed connection can travel onward.

This is **not an extra dial** on Abundent's eleven-dial interface, a new universal identity plane, a roaming megachurch, or a protocol controlling all owners. The topper is a reversible composition of invitations.

**Source status, physical observation, human participation, and local consequence are separate histories.** A song's recurrence is not evidence of an event. A shared verse does not mean two churches share a doctrine. A reported gift is not a recipient-confirmed gift.

## 3. Revival's actual source frontier, not a magical Bible API

- [Revival 010 — First World](https://github.com/the-static-collective/revival/blob/main/docs/FIRST_WORLD.md) proves a locally rendered, standalone **Genesis 1:1–3** Scripture world with source/annotation/derivation/fog, inspectable receipts and selectable renderings.
- [Revival 011 — World Places](https://github.com/the-static-collective/revival/blob/main/docs/WORLD_PLACES.md) adds `revival://` projection addresses; *address is not geography, referent or truth*.
- [Revival 012 — Earned Names](https://github.com/the-static-collective/revival/blob/main/docs/EARNED_NAMES.md) gives source-aligned lexeme names but keeps semantic sense, referent and world type visibly unresolved.
- [Revival PR #13](https://github.com/the-static-collective/revival/pull/13) is a **draft/open owner-specific external DOOR PACKET receiver for Upper Room only**. It accepts a restricted `static.door-packet/0.1` and holds with `revivalAddress: null` and `authority: null`. The tree, a song, and a concert **must not impersonate that schema**.

**Bible references for Jubilee** — Leviticus 25, Isaiah 61, Luke 4, Acts 2 and 4 — are human-curated thematic Scripture *citations*. They do **not** currently establish source-backed Revival world entries because the demonstrated Revival corpus is the Genesis-opening proof. The host can read them from independently chosen legitimate Bibles; the digital topper must say `CITATION_ONLY / SOURCE_UNRESOLVED` until Revival independently imports and verifies the required source editions and licenses.

**First possible actual digital Scripture door:** verify one Revival-generated Gen.1.1 source address from the existing standalone build and offer a click to inspect it. Never construct a `revival://` address from an arbitrary typed reference, program theme, song lyric or QR label.

## 4. What the festival looks like

A patchwork traveling village, deliberately built from reclaimed and repairable things: lanterns, hand-painted doors, old radio chimes, a visible wooden/copper Giving Tree, small music tents and a portable Scripture stand. *The spectacle is a hospitable invitation, not proof that a spiritual event was mechanically produced.*

One host day can have a breakfast and service opportunity, open afternoon family festival, locally curated evening worship, and optional night Giving Tree handoff. All stages are optional. All aid remains available without a requirement to attend worship or profess belief.

A card on the tree can offer **a paper door first**. A printed QR might resolve a standard, published local page; a song cue might propose a read-only view. No one needs an account, phone, network, microphone, or participation score. The host retains final local choices on safety, child participation, microphone, doctrine and event logistics.

Each town keeps a local branch. The caravan may carry **one consented artifact** to the next town. Town B may offer to answer a need from Town A, but proposal, private introduction, local acceptance, reported delivery and recipient confirmation remain distinct.

## 7. Hostile questions before promotion

1. QR says `Luke 4`, Revival has no matching owned corpus: **HOLD_UNRESOLVED**, never fabricate an address or source bytes.
2. Printed Gen.1.1 QR is scanned twice: separate observed occurrences, no duplicate consequence.
3. A performance contains verse-like language: keep it **song/interpretation**, not Scripture witness or encoded source.
4. A church declines, or two host traditions disagree: neither gets silently listed, harmonized or compelled.
5. Someone needs food but declines prayer: provide eligible help without religious condition.
6. A helper reports delivery but the recipient has not confirmed: remain **REPORTED**, not **CONFIRMED**.
7. A minor or vulnerable neighbor is pictured, named or described: no automatic public tag, photograph, livestream, archive or source import.
8. A contribution permits physical CD handoff but not public broadcast: carry only that approved scope.
9. A user withdraws a public invitation: block owner-controlled fresh matching/reuse; do not promise copies disappeared elsewhere.
10. Sensor or song similarity points at a citation: observation/encounter candidate only; no doctrine or person inference.
11. Upper Room's exact Door Packet is presented: only the separately owned source-specific Revival receiver may decide to HOLD it.
12. No network, power, phone or special hardware: paper, human welcome and safe host-curated gathering remain sufficient.

## 8. Executable software descendant

[JUBILEE-001](../jubilee/README.md) implements the synthetic three-card T0 evaluator and an interactive three-town rehearsal. The original synthetic evaluator keeps its own null source fields.

This earns software and a verified local source artifact, not a live town, authenticated consent, real receipt or T1–T3 field proof. The internal demo/notebook schemas do not replace any owner's native contract. See [the field-kit checklist](../jubilee/field/README.md) for prepared tools versus actual human evidence still needed.
