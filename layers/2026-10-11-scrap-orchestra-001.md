## Changed first gate

Previously FIELD-CELL prioritized soldering an RC soil/copper circuit. That remains a valid **later** physics experiment. The newly discovered intact salvage permits an earlier, simpler and independently measurable physical-computation interface:

    [Known source tone] -> [air & particular room] -> [laptop microphone]

Follow [STATIC-OS user-specific build sheet](https://github.com/the-static-collective/static-os/blob/experiment/field-cell-001-scrap-bench/docs/SCRAP-ORCHESTRA-001.md); no soldering or purchase is required. ## Harm and consent boundaries

Do not probe a mains-powered wireless doorbell receiver or A/V receiver interior, disconnect safety grounds, repurpose mismatched DC adapters, short laptop batteries, pull capacitors from microwaves/CRTs/flash circuits, or connect a speaker-output terminal to a laptop microphone input. Preserve personal files on old computers and avoid connecting unpatched network gear to the public internet.

Household sound may contain people, private conversations or unintended presence information. Any capture or published room relation is explicitly opt-in, locally bounded, time-limited and inspectable; no perpetual room recorder.
