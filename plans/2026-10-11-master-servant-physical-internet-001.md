## Mission

Give material things interoperable ways to originate, transmit, discover, and carry attributable digital artifacts: Green Grammar fractal sigil QRs; deliberate data embedded in authorized audio derivatives; song-to-song cue sequences; lawful radio; light pulses; controlled soil/copper experiments; certified power-line networking; isolated old telephone pairs.

The **Master** is the declaration of the interoperable grammar, evidence requirements, and acceptance tests—not a central agent commanding hardware. Each **Servant** owns its separate carrier, decoder, losses, and provenance. A receiver retains local authority.

~~~text
source song / object / human / source edition
    -> portable Portal Envelope V0 + optional signed manifest
    -> SIGIL | SONG | SCORE | BEACON | LANTERN | ROOT | VEIN | GHOSTWIRE
    -> medium-specific receiving instrument
    -> FIELD-SCORE observation and encounter candidate
    -> HOLD / human inspection / refusal / explicit admission
    -> reLATTE crossing where separately supported
    -> Abundent / Workbench local consequence
    -> replayable, non-collapsed histories
~~~

**Essential non-collapses:** DOOR != CROSSING; SIGNAL != IDENTITY; DETECTION != CONSENT; CONTENT ID != PHYSICAL EVENT ID; TWO CARRIERS != TWO INDEPENDENT WITNESSES; SENSOR AMPLITUDE != INFORMATION; COMPOSITION != RENDER; CUE != SOURCE TRUTH; PROPOSAL != SELECTION; RECEPTION != ADMISSION; SIGNATURE != TRUTH; RECEIPT != AUTHORITY; BRANCH != CANON.

## Portable Portal Envelope V0 (proposal; not registered schema)

~~~json
{
  "schema": "sc.portal/v0",
  "portal_id": "portal:orchard-001",
  "artifact_ref": "artifact:singing-postcard-001",
  "proposal": "PRESENT",
  "manifest_digest": "sha256:<manifest-digest>",
  "sequence_ref": {"score_id": "three-doors-001", "cue_id": "song-b"},
  "nonce": "<presented-or-occurrence-id>",
  "expiry": null
}
~~~

A tiny sonic/radio/optical payload can carry a compact pointer to a manifest, but that does NOT mean the manifest bytes are available offline. Preserve a stable artifact reference separately from native carrier identity and from observation occurrence identity. A checksum detects corruption; signed manifest verification requires an appropriate key/trust profile and proves neither real-world truth nor permission. No wire-level EXECUTE operation. Candidate proposal verbs: PRESENT, REQUEST_VIEW, QUEUE_CANDIDATE, PROPOSE_DIAL, MEASURE. Explicit local human policy is required before links, remote fetch, effects or device actions.

## SIGIL — a QR wearing Green Grammar

The QR core stays standards-readable and has an untouched quiet zone and locator/timing structures. Separate deterministic SVG fractal art surrounds it. Seed ornamentation from the stable manifest digest; if any branch, gap, or ring claims to represent an actual relation, require separately sourced evidence. Keep a plaintext fallback. Test printing, rotations, small sizes, camera models, color loss and ink bleed. Later, a native fractal-sigil decoder is an independent proposal, never misrepresented as a standard QR.

Use the Green Grammar seed anatomy: kernel / carrier / decoder / mutation / required relations. The visual grammar is meaning-bearing only insofar as its claimed relations can be checked.

## SONG × SCORE — a playlist that can speak

Preserve three distinct truths:
1. **Declared composition** — a LemonPRESS-style score sidecar names cue payloads, timestamps, intended editions and roles.
2. **Rendered acoustic material** — an independently hashed derivative master actually carries an audible signal; a decoder tests that signal in air.
3. **Observed encounter sequence** — actual hearing/decoding events, uncertainty, loss, order, repeat and voluntary user participation.

Generate a legal/authorized derivative audio master rather than claiming an upstream Suno song was secretly encoded. Begin with an explicitly testable audio modem cue, later test masking and robust watermarking as separate experiments. A song may open a proposed UI door, not execute code. A playlist may act as an interactive score, but missing or reordered songs may never be invented by a parser.

## Radio / light / ground / power / phone safety and proof

**Radio:** GHoT currently has unmerged receive-only driver and attention specimens; CI uses fake RTL-SDR. No transmitter and no actual RF field evidence are implied. Radio TX/RX requires distinct authorized hardware and regulatory tests.

**Light:** safe room-scale screen/camera first, then low-intensity LED/photodiode with measured ambient light, timing loss and distances. TranchNode luminous-terrain research is a future coordinate, not a live network.

**Ground:** only isolated battery-powered benchtop soil sample with dry/wet/sham controls, measured signal-to-noise, power and attenuation. No Tesla-coil high voltage; no mains, utility ground, buried service, pipes, lightning system, tree roots or neighboring land. An observed voltage by itself does not establish propagating communication.

**Power wiring:** certified networking adapters only. No custom signal coupling to energized wiring.

**Telephone wiring:** verified disconnected owned low-voltage spool/run only, never live telco equipment or an untested abandoned pair. Legacy ringing voltages may be dangerous.
