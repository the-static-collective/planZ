# THE GARDEN — MASTER SHARD 001

**planZ · 2026-10-21 · DRAFT / HELD · NOT CANON · NO PROPERTY RIGHTS OR PERMISSIONS CONFERRED**

**Working name:** The Garden — possible name for a local food commons and an independently stewarded growing/hospitality node. No grocery, landowner, family member, venue, or partner has adopted the name or approved participation.

**Relation:** Sibling to [COMMON TABLE WASECA-001 (draft PR #23)](https://github.com/the-static-collective/planZ/pull/23). Common Table concerns food, arts, cards and a possible community venue; this shard asks whether one existing residential place can become a carefully bounded physical node. Neither project requires the other to succeed.

> **North star:** A home need not stop being a home to become a place where useful things grow.
>
> **Order of concern:** SHELTER → CONSENT → STEWARDSHIP → CULTIVATION → HOSPITALITY → VOLUNTARY RETURN.

## 1. The initiating particular

A privately held family-connected duplex in the Waseca region may be available for a *conversation* about housing continuity, a garden, visits, hospitality, community meals, workshops and possible connections to the regional grocery effort. The landholder(s) have not yet approved a use, transfer or new entity. Any future remote parcel or deed is an **unverified separate research lead**.

**Public privacy rule:** No address, parcel ID, map pin, ownership names, detailed family circumstances, vehicle location or site pictures enter this public repository. A private owner-authorized feasibility packet, if desired, is separate.

| Status | Claim |
| --- | --- |
| HUMAN REPORT | A candidate family-connected residential property exists as an idea to discuss. |
| PROPOSAL | Secure home + optional growing and hospitality + a voluntary food-commons return route. |

Informal expectations about who a property was intended for do **not** establish ownership, a right of occupancy, or an enforceable inheritance.

## 2. The Garden covenant (aspirational only)

> The Garden shall first preserve shelter, belonging, privacy and a workable household.
>
> From that stability, its stewards may extend cultivation, hospitality, learning and fellowship.
>
> No invitation to a community shall silently extinguish the rights of those who call the place home.
>
> Owners, lawful occupants and willing stewards retain their separate powers to say yes, no, not yet, or stop.

This is a statement of intent, **not** a deed restriction, trust term, lease, legal covenant or authorization to open the site.

## 3. Three layers, three different questions

```text
OWNERSHIP & SUCCESSION: Who holds title? Who may convey it? What happens later?
       │ owner-authorized legal agreement (if any)
HOME & OCCUPANCY: Who can live there, use the premises, and rely on stability?
       │ narrow site-use envelope; independent permissions
THE GARDEN ACTIVITIES: cultivation / workshops / food / visits / other uses
       │ voluntary offers, no implicit property rights
ABUNDENT / RIGHT HANDS: cards, approved information, opt-in return
```

A public card cannot convey a land interest. A trustee is not automatically a venue operator. A house is not automatically a campground. A beneficiary designation is not automatically an occupancy guarantee.

## 4. Two parallel tracks

### A. Family security: make the intended benefit legible

**First owner conversation:** What outcome is the property meant to serve? A home now, a future inheritance, a tenancy, housing support, a gift, retained income, or a combination? Who bears taxes, maintenance, improvements and repairs? What if circumstances change?

With a Minnesota estate-planning and real-estate professional, compare:

| Possible structure | What it might accomplish | What it does NOT automatically do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Retain existing title + written occupancy terms | Clarify household use, duration, expenses and responsibilities | Transfer property or promise inheritance |
| Owners' revocable living trust + separate housing terms | Potential estate continuity and named successor management | Make a beneficiary's interest irrevocable while settlor retains control; shelter property from all creditors |
| Other trust, deed or legally defined future interest | Address more specific binding family objectives | Avoid tax, mortgage, creditor, homestead or inheritance analysis |
| Owner-granted limited site-use agreement | Permit a bounded Garden activity without donating land | Permit business activity disallowed by zoning or insurance |
| Community land trust, as a later *distinct* model | Preserve affordable housing through nonprofit land stewardship | Convert a family property automatically into a public community garden |

Minnesota statutes: [trust creation §501C.0401](https://www.revisor.mn.gov/statutes/cite/501C.0401); [settlor powers §501C.0604](https://www.revisor.mn.gov/statutes/cite/501C.0604); [creditors §501C.0505](https://www.revisor.mn.gov/statutes/cite/501C.0505); [community land trusts §462A.31](https://www.revisor.mn.gov/statutes/cite/462A.31). These are orientation leads, not site-specific legal conclusions.

**Working preference, not a recommendation or commitment:** establish owner intentions and residence stability *before* considering any deed transfer; pilot a very limited use only if independently authorized.

### B. Site program: grow without displacing the home

| Door | Candidate activity | Evidence required first |
| --- | --- | --- |
| HOME | Ordinary dwelling / family base | Lawful occupancy terms, habitability, finance and insurance |
| GROW | Raised beds, seed exchange, soil learning | Owner consent, water/soil and land-use review, steward |
| SHARE | Surplus produce offered to willing recipients | Food handling and custody terms; partner acceptance |
| GATHER | Small workshops, fellowship, acoustic music | Permitted use, neighbors, safety, parking, host approval |
| REST | Short stay, RV/camping or lodging *research* | Property-specific zoning, lodging/tax/health/fire/insurance determinations **before booking** |
| MAKE | Small creative, practical or youth learning activities | Space suitability, consent, supervision and safeguarding |
| RETURN | Optional Abundent card/page and printed invitations | Approval of public details, privacy boundaries, revocation method |

**One door failing must not collapse the others.** The home remains valuable even if all public-facing uses are refused.

## 6. Physical, legal and operating gates

Before any outward-facing activity, confirm with permission whether the actual parcel is in Waseca City or a county/township jurisdiction. [The City's zoning guidance](https://www.ci.waseca.mn.us/1227/Zoning-Ordinance) makes district and accessory/conditional-use distinctions; it does not preapprove this parcel.

Ask separately about: two-unit residential occupancy; gardening and sales; access/parking; food donation vs preparation; temporary events; fire/building/egress; lodging; number and kind of RV or camping units; sanitation/water; insurance and neighborhood impacts. Do not assume small scale is exempt.

**Cost discipline:** establish baseline property expenses and obligations privately; then estimate *incremental* garden/program spending from quotes. Essential housing must still function with **zero** visitors, donations, rentals or volunteer labor. Keep household, commercial, aid and artist-payment ledgers separate.

**Stop if:** an owner declines; occupancy becomes precarious; zoning/insurance denies a use; volunteers inherit unbounded maintenance; a guest or child-safety rule fails; public data exposes residents; or funds cannot be reconciled.

## 7. Earned stages, not an automatic rollout

| Stage | Bounded task | Owner / gate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **0 — LISTEN** | Present one-page purpose; ask family owner(s) what security they want and whether study is welcome | Owners may freely decline; no request to transfer property |
| **1 — UNDERSTAND** | Private title/occupancy/insurance/estate and land-use feasibility packet; legal consultation if invited | Owner-authorized scope; no public parcel data |
| **2 — GROW ONE THING** | One low-cost approved garden bed/seed activity **or** merely a planning session | Written boundary, budget cap, named caretaker, lawful use |
| **3 — ADD ONE DOOR** | Optional produce exchange, workshop, acoustic event or lodging *feasibility review*, not all at once | Separate permissions, safety and economics |
| **4 — RETURN / DECIDE** | Record what happened, costs, burdens, objections, consent, and whether to revise/stop | Property owners and participants keep decision rights |
| **5 — REPLICATE IF EARNED** | Offer a standard site-stewardship agreement to *another willing owner* | Independent property authority and local verification |

A possible second deed or property is **NOT VERIFIED**. If located, it begins a new, jurisdiction-specific chain of title and stewardship review. It is not added to any trust by this document.

## 8. Decision matrix

| Matter | Actual authority |
| --- | --- |
| Trust, deed, inheritance, sale | Title-holder(s), governing law and appropriately retained professionals |
| Housing/occupancy | Actual owners, occupants' rights and executed instruments |
| Garden, event, short-stay invitation | Site owner/steward, law, permits, insurer; not a web platform |
| Grocery affiliation | The grocery initiative's authorized decision-makers |
| Public card | Consenting source and local steward |
| Withdrawal / stop | Those holding the underlying rights; safety officials where applicable |

**Failure fixtures:** owner permits garden but no public guests → private growing only; camping denied → close that door, retain HOME/GROW; grocer declines partnership/name → remain independent; expenses exceed cap → stop; household privacy exposed → retract publication immediately.
