# PLANZ SHARD — COMMUNITY-BREATH-025
*2026-10-22 · proposed cross-repo plan, not canon, adopted infrastructure or deployment*

## North star

Make the Collective's still-useful instruments **available as voluntary, attributable offers** for actual neighboring people and organizations. Never move an organ's authority, owner data, rights or implementation into a master platform.

> One person names a concrete possibility. Two people consent to a bounded next step. Something useful may happen. Its report is separated from another person's confirmation. The relationship has a way to return.

The physical manifestation is **The Neighbor Door**: beautifully printed, paper-first OFFER / NEED / BUILD / CONNECT / STEWARD / RETURN cards. Anyone can participate without an account, QR scan, donation or AI service. A later online door on Abundent can follow only when published with explicit permissions. No existing Abundent production code is modified by this plan.

## Separate currents — CARE and CULTURE

CARE: need, offer, accept, act, report, confirm or dispute. Reporting is not confirming, and a payment or donation does not stand in for delivery.

CULTURE: musical seed, independent contribution, creator permission, editorial decision, release or withdrawal. Creative consent never follows automatically from care participation. No song or documentary promotion using a neighbor's personal need without fresh, independent permission.

Both may inhabit the same community celebration. They remain separate lawful decisions.
