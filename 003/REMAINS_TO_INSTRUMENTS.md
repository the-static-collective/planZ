# PLANZ-003 — REMAINS → INSTRUMENTS

## Question

What if the residue of completed work is not merely evidence to archive, but material that can be made playable again?

PLANZ-003 introduces one bounded answer:

> A finished artifact may expose **addressable organs** that can be proposed as instruments while preserving source lineage and withholding selection authority.

This does not mean every finished artifact should be reused.
It does not mean planZ decides what survives.
It does not mean extraction transfers ownership, authorship, authority, or project-local control.

## Placement

PLANZ-003 stacks on PLANZ-002.

```text
PlanRecord
  → SenseFrame
  → MutationCandidate
  → explicit artifact witness
  → explicit organ witness
  → InstrumentProposal
  → separate human/project admission
  → external play / rendering / composition
  → returned consequence + provenance
```

The important seam is between **proposal** and **play**.

## Terms

### Finished artifact

A source treated as complete enough that its original production loop has ended.

"Finished" is historical posture, not metaphysical finality.

### Organ

A bounded, addressable behavior or structure explicitly identified inside or derived from an artifact.

Examples:

- timing contour;
- lyric geometry;
- motion grammar;
- transition behavior;
- camera gesture;
- harmonic/rhythmic contour;
- layout topology;
- decision pattern;
- verification procedure;
- interaction mechanic;
- narrative beat structure.

An organ is not the entire source and does not inherit the source's authority.

### Instrument proposal

A provenance-preserving suggestion that an organ could be exposed for play by another system.

It is not executable permission.
It is not selection.
It is not a transfer of ownership.
It is not a claim that the destination system can actually render it.

## Authority split

planZ may:

- preserve source lineage;
- record explicit organ descriptions;
- derive stable proposal identifiers;
- state candidate destination capabilities;
- preserve refusals and residue;
- receive later consequence receipts.

planZ may not:

- infer that reuse is desirable;
- infer ownership or license;
- select an instrument for use;
- render media;
- force destination systems to accept an organ;
- rewrite project-local authority;
- claim a new artifact exists before a returned witness proves it.

These are candidate destinations, not dependencies of the v0 engine.

## Refusal laws

```text
IMPLICIT FEATURE != ORGAN
ORGAN != INSTRUMENT
INSTRUMENT != ADMISSION
ADMISSION != PERFORMANCE
PERFORMANCE != PROVENANCE
PROVENANCE != AUTHORITY
ANCESTRY != OWNERSHIP
SIMILARITY != LINEAGE
```

No organ is emitted unless it is explicitly named in the artifact witness.

No new artifact is inferred merely because a proposal was emitted.

## First real witness

The target end-to-end witness is intentionally physical in the sense of consequence:

```text
one finished artifact
  → one explicit organ
  → one instrument proposal
  → one external playable implementation
  → one human selection/performance
  → one genuinely new artifact
  → one returned provenance receipt
```

A failed or refused play remains valuable residue and must not be erased.

## Completion law

> **Finished work may become playable history, but history does not play itself.**
