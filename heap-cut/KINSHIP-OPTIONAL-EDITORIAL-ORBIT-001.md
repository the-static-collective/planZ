# KINSHIP × GROCERY — OPTIONAL EDITORIAL ORBIT 001
**Dated proposal · 2026-10-21 · HEAP-CUT-001 · independent station permission required**

## Why this particular connection is promising

A regionally operating radio station may help people **hear** about community food access, public meetings, requests for nonfinancial material help, and verified work that local producers and neighbors elect to share. But an FM transmitter is not a bidirectional delivery network, a household directory, a co-op operator, or a claim on volunteers. Kinship Radio's existing community calendar and independent editorial staff already provide real-world, station-controlled doorways. These are **public station capabilities, not consent to this project**.

The experimental Kinship code is located in [Static Live](https://github.com/the-static-collective/static-live). [KINSHIP-006](https://github.com/the-static-collective/static-live/pull/31) is a review-only, unmerged bridge from a verified signed *public need history* to a **HELD** station-producer candidate. [KINSHIP-007](https://github.com/the-static-collective/static-live/pull/33) explores separate permission to display, quote, broadcast or reuse submitted supporter media. These are neither station-approved, nor live integrations.

## Three separately permissioned crossings

| Door | Source authority | Kinship-side human authority | Return |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Community calendar** | Actual organizer approves event facts and public contact | Station's established submission and editorial policy | Public listing **if** station accepts; no automatically inferred attendance |
| **Neighbor-help radio porch** | Local aid steward, current beneficiary consent and source verification; eligible nonmoney resources only | Producer rechecks freshness, audience safety, local relevance and permissions; separate station decision | Respondents use an organizer-controlled safe contact; recipient-side confirmation remains with the aid process |
| **Outcome story** | Person(s) whose work and story it is separately license/use-authorize the media | Kinship editor chooses whether, how, and when to produce or air | Owner-approved attribution, publication and optional independently verified response |

## KINSHIP-006 reuse: what may cross?

A human-curated, source-referenced **public-safe HELD candidate** may cross from an eligible local resource request toward a station producer. If a future approved integration uses KINSHIP-006 specifically, its own source rules still apply: complete signed chain, no missing revisions or forged material, stable key, withdrawn-state rejection, reviewed current consent and safe route. A signature proves source continuity, **not beneficiary identity**; a radio pack is not an on-air event.

Do **not** pass private prayer requests, home addresses, donor information, financial/payment records, sensitive beneficiary stories, direct volunteer assignments, or live tracking into this orbit. The existing Kinship giving system remains completely separate.

**Radio outreach must not be a condition of receiving groceries or assistance.** A person may refuse faith programming, station contact or a public story without losing access to food or services otherwise available through the real operator.

## Review / technical limitations

- The [living map](network-map.html) is an embedded offline snapshot to show **possible relationships**; it is not a synchronized radio API or a service-area coverage map.
- Machine-readable source: [kinship-candidate-overlay-v0.json](kinship-candidate-overlay-v0.json), a distinct overlay referencing the main [network passport](network-passport-v0.json).
- In-house structure/adversarial checks: [kinship-map.test.mjs](kinship-map.test.mjs).
- The public station calendar explicitly invites event announcements by organizations; that standing invitation is **not** a promise to list or broadcast any proposal.
- The [station's volunteer/ambassador information](https://kinshipradio.org/main/ambassador-and-volunteer-faqs/) does not authorize recruitment or assignment by the grocery, planZ or our card system.
