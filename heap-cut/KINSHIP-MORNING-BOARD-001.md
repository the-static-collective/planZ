# KINSHIP-MORNING-BOARD-001 — SOMETHING USEFUL TO SAY TODAY

**Human-origin observation (Oct 21, 2026):** Kinship's morning show sometimes needs more immediately usable, locally meaningful content. Proposed answer: **a rotating, producer-controlled Morning Board built from the live Right Hands card network.** This is a research/development proposal only; Kinship Radio has not requested, accepted, scheduled or broadcast this instrument.

## Why morning radio is the right doorway

Kinship's **Ryan and LaDonna** morning format publicly includes recurring audience-friendly slots: "What Day Is It?", "Question for You", "Storytime", "Bible Quiz", "Encouraging Your Soul", and "Just One More Thing" (show archive examples below). A June 2026 lineup announcement framed Ryan and LaDonna's morning partnership around listener interaction and strengthening community relationships across southern Minnesota and northern Iowa.

Do not interpret an observer's "needs content" impression as authorization or knowledge of station operational problems. This proposal aims to reduce **human preparation effort**, preserve original programming style and increase **locally useful choices**, not replace announcers.

### What the producer would actually see

```text
MORNING BOARD — prepared at 6:20 AM (illustrative, not actual station schedule)
  SOURCE CUT / VERIFIED AS OF [time] / all candidate items HELD
  ├─ 20-second SINGLE CARD       : one reviewed help or event possibility
  ├─ 45-second NEIGHBOR MOMENT    : two current cards + call to current-state web door
  ├─ 90-second COMMUNITY ROUNDUP  : up to three cards + human-selected question
  ├─ TODAY'S QUESTION            : "What's a small skill you could share locally?"
  ├─ RETURN / THANK-YOU          : only owner-permitted, independently confirmed outcomes
  └─ NO-OPEN-CARDS FALLBACK      : useful non-urgent invitation; no invented needs

  PRODUCER:
    source and permission checks → EDIT / HOLD / REFUSE
    chooses slot and exact words → may air or choose not to

  LISTENER:
    current online card ID/URL → independent steward-managed response
```

**One source network, several possible speaking shapes.** The system does not need to produce more generic synthetic material. It helps a human host select one true, time-bounded local particular.

## Three read lengths

These are NOT station-approved scripts. Use fictitious categories and no actual requests unless sourced/authorized.

**20 seconds — One Card**

> "A local community project is exploring a need for a food pickup volunteer. Details and current availability live on its own community card page. Check the current card board before responding—needs change during the day."

**45 seconds — Neighbors at Work**

> "One thing I love about our region is how many small acts can add up. Imagine a grower with extra produce, a neighbor with an open pickup route, and a community kitchen with room to receive. Our local card-board experiment explores helping those relationships find each other. What kind of practical help could you offer this week? The card board shows which requests are actually current."

**90 seconds — Morning Board / question-and-return**

A station-editor-approved, human-produced rundown could combine: opening question; **one to three independently authorized current opportunities**; one previously verified and separately permissioned outcome; a repeated current-state online doorway. The host is free to ignore the packet, write original wording or choose no card at all.

These examples are demo copy, not statements that the fictional opportunities currently exist or that Kinship has endorsed the concept.

## The actual killer feature: content shaped by incoming responses

```
  MONDAY                 TUESDAY                     WEDNESDAY
  Need card opened       Listeners respond online   Steward reports/recipient confirms
  Producer picks cut     Card PAUSED while screened Card FILLED / WITHDRAWN
       │                       │                           │
  radio snapshot A      online card current-state     radio snapshot B
                                                      drops closed card
  Other cards enter → new editorial choices and potential "one good thing" stories
```

This is **not an automatic on-air content engine**. Radio producers choose if and when to use a cut. The web steward keeps the card current and approves what may be published; the station approves each program use separately. Last week's recording may still circulate: the stable online ID must show the updated state.

## Morning producer workflow

1. **Before programming:** local stewards recheck their own public card status, expiration, safe online route, and distinct optional radio consent.
2. **Source projection:** proposal generator emits a small bounded, priority-unsorted packet with card ID, source revision, as-of time, expiry and category. No household/contact data, money-only solicitation or raw beneficiary story.
3. **Human station selection:** a station producer reviews freshness, station fit, risk, accuracy and exact speakable copy. A "ready" computer status is neither station approval nor evidence of airing.
4. **At the microphone:** host may use one card, several, or none; include one brief "check online for current status" cue. Keep an immediate skip/fallback if the card has filled or been withdrawn.
5. **Afterward:** station may independently confirm actual airing. Any local online offers remain under local steward control. Recipient or owner separately confirms any eventual fulfillment. Next edition rechecks source and removes closed/stale items.

### Different priorities for host and steward

**Station** needs short, speakable, varied, verified community material and the right to refuse at any moment.

**Grocery/aid steward** needs correct incoming responses, safety, bounded workloads, and the ability to pause the card when enough volunteers have responded.

**Participant** needs no pressure to be named on radio or join a religious organization to access any separately available community assistance.

**planZ** needs a source-linked account of what was *proposed*, what was *accepted by the actual owner*, and what independently happened afterward.

## Hard edges for pilot

- No real cards or live broadcast are produced here. The existing [offline rotating-card simulation](kinship-live-queue.html) is synthetic.
- The dashboard may suggest 20/45/90-second lengths, but never promise exact read time without a real announcer test.
- No address, individual beneficiary story, private prayer request, donor information, contact list, financial solicitation, audience surveillance, or emergency dispatch on the card projection.
- No faith-based participation requirement for grocery/community assistance.
- Do not treat commercial grocery product ads as unpaid neighbor help. Radio sponsorship/advertising rules remain entirely station-owned.
- Do not automate content into a live station show. Kinship's own staff would approve any future adapter, editorial workflow, airtime and integration.
- If no verified cards are currently available, do not invent a local need. Offer an approved generic participation question or skip the slot.
