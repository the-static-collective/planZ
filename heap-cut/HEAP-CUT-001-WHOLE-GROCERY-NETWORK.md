# HEAP-CUT-001 — THE WHOLE GROCERY NETWORK CROSSES

**planZ proposal / 2026-10-21 / DRAFT · HELD FOR REAL-WORLD OWNERSHIP AND EVIDENCE**

**Subject:** A candidate Waseca community grocery **network**, considered as one complete, source-linked crossing through the Static Collective's existing heap of plans. This is neither a grocery's endorsed plan nor a claim that a store, co-op, food network, Garden venue, or partnership currently operates.

> **Change the unit of analysis:** Not one grocery website. Not one handoff. Not one shop building. **The entire network of people, capabilities, custody, governance, exchange, consent and return is the thing we attempt to carry across.**

## 0 — What "the whole network" means

The **Network Passport** is a dated snapshot of a *proposed constellation*: distinguishable organizations/roles, source evidence, physical and information flows, permissions, refusal points, missing capacities, and independent return receipts. It is **one addressable planning object**, not one legal person, database, purse, trust or controlling authority.

The system can make the proposal coherent **without** pretending the actors have joined it. Every change is a new passport version with its own evidence and unresolved differences. A human may carry the passport from one conversation to another; the passport must never turn an introduction into a commitment.

### The three different meanings of "complete"

1. **Complete as a question:** All essential *kinds* of actors/relationships and failure paths are named, including UNKNOWN. This is achievable now as a map.
2. **Complete as an approved pilot:** Each indispensable real role and custody handoff has a properly authorized human owner, a legal/financial/safety review, and a bounded test plan. **Not achieved.**
3. **Complete as a working network:** Independently witnessed real operation plus exception handling and settlement, across multiple authorized actors and repeatable cycles. **Not achieved.** One successful grocery drop-off would be a useful *edge witness*, not proof of a whole network.

**A missing node is a first-class answer.** A refusal keeps the whole proposed picture legible without appointing a replacement.

## 1 — Proposed network anatomy (not a partnership roster)

A complete *model* needs the following essential systems:

| Organ | Essential question | External decision-maker / proof |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **VOICE / GOVERNANCE** | Who actually owns the grocery vision, membership rules, pilot decisions and right to say no? | Legitimate grocery organizers / prospective cooperative, not planZ |
| **PRODUCE / SUPPLY** | Who can offer what quantities, at what prices, seasons, times and standards? | Independent growers, distributors and procurement managers |
| **CUSTODY / SAFETY** | Who receives, inspects, cools/stores, counts, handles spoilage and stops unsafe food? | Licensed/qualified food operator and relevant regulators |
| **PLACE / OPERATIONS** | What space, equipment, utilities, accessibility, labor, insurance and operating procedure exist? | Venue or property owner and authorized operator |
| **COMMERCE / ACCOUNTING** | How do retail sales, taxes, inventory, supplier payment, returns and liabilities reconcile? | The actual retail operator and its accounting/POS controls |
| **ACCESS / HOUSEHOLDS** | Who can actually buy, collect, receive or request access support, and how are outcomes checked? | Participants, customer service operators, delivery stewards |
| **AUDIT / FAILURE / RETURN** | Who receives complaints, refuses an unsafe move, corrects a mismatch and confirms outcomes? | Actual source/recipient/operator, each within their scope |

These are **required fields in a network model**, not claims that each institution or function already exists. Some roles may be held by the same qualified operator only if verified.

Distinct optional orbit systems (never prerequisites or implied members):

- **The Garden (THE-GARDEN-001 / planZ PR #25):** possible home-protected cultivation node. It is independent and unapproved by the site's owners. No property location or family names belong here.
- **Common Table (COMMON-TABLE-WASECA-001 / planZ PR #23):** potential separate food, arts, stage, gallery, café and learning interface; it does not appoint the grocery or control its money.
- **Neighbor aid / PENNY:** separately consented help, rides or equipment, with independent charity/voluntary-work ledgers. No conversion to co-op money, equity, volunteer assignments or recipient profiles.
- **Abundent Field (FIELD-001 / planZ PR #24):** proposed public-safe card discovery, printable offers/questions and optional returns. Not a co-op website or backend by default.
- **Churches, community groups, other facilities:** potential *invitation targets* only if properly approached and willing, not affiliated because they share a region or mission.

**Core topology:** producer → authorized receiving → storage/inventory → retailer or approved distribution → shopper/household → feedback/exception return. Governance, safety, facility, commercial accounting and accessibility cross-cut every physical link. Optional aid is a distinct permissioned route, not a shortcut around retail or food-safety custody.

A transition is not a calendar prediction. It requires **evidence from the person or organization with the relevant authority**. A graph export, high score, or a model's confident interpretation cannot advance it.

**Key insight:** N1 can be complete **as a map containing unfinished business**. No participant has to consent to the map's existence. N2–N5 necessarily belong to people outside planZ. A refusal at N2 is a legitimate, recordable crossing result.

## 5 — The first full-network rehearsal (only after appropriate local review)

Do **not** conflate a simulated network with a real service.

**Fixture:** fictional seasonal apples from one hypothetical grower, one receiving party, one retail operator and a household; safety, purchase, inventory, delivery and exception decisions are separately simulated. Simultaneously show *other necessary roles as blank/unknown*, rather than telling an audience all network roles have been fulfilled.

**Happy path:** produce offer → buyer acceptance → receiving inspection → inventory → retailer sale → lawful pickup/delivery → shopper receipt → ledger reconciliation → return.

**Failure path:** refrigeration/quality fails or delivery is disputed → qualified owner rejects/holds → goods and money resolution remain with the correct operators → recipient-side confirmation/dispute → public-safe outcome. No forced completion, no hidden volunteer assignment.

**Network-level question:** If the grower, transport volunteer or hosting site says no, can the *remaining network* honestly state its status without inventing another participant? If the co-op declines to use the prototype, does the study stop while the surrounding independent community connections remain intact?

**Proof against vanity:** one crate delivered = one witnessed path, NOT a proven cooperative business model or complete network. A whole-network proof must separately address governance, dependable supply, food safety, premises, money settlement, access and exceptional cases, plus repetition across independently participating parties.

## 6 — Public-safe network passport contract

The accompanying `network-passport-v0.json` is a **proposal fixture**. It must contain:

- provenance links to planZ siblings and current field brief;
- all required organ names with actual-owner status **UNKNOWN** and the exact question to resolve;
- optional orbits that can be refused without disabling the core grocery;
- a proposed graph of links, with no presumed legal participants;
- separate commerce, aid and nonmonetary contribution lanes;
- per-threshold evidence requirements and unmet gates;
- only non-identifying roles; no household or private property information.

**Revisions:** Preserve original passport, exact source version and changed relation; do not overwrite a declined node with a replacement or upgrade `UNKNOWN` because a conversation occurred. An eventual real receipt links an operator-held source, what was observed, and exactly which claim it supports. Maintain a default-private evidence annex outside public GitHub.

### What the passport emphatically cannot do

- create co-op membership, incorporation, ownership, credentials, duties, contracts or approval;
- create a commercial ledger, receive charitable money or assess tax;
- establish food-safety compliance, lawfully operate a retail store, or certify a property use;
- designate a family-owned property for public access;
- claim an endorsement by the Coalition, grocery organizers, any church or local government;
- identify beneficiaries, monitor scans/attendees or expose private data;
- imply that everyone has accepted one global governing body.

## 7 — Decisions the real grocery network must own

1. **Ownership of the opportunity:** Is the grocery exploring membership, demand, site, finance, producer relationships or some other next question?
2. **Authority:** Who may speak for the grocery organization and authorize a pilot, if any?
3. **Current capabilities:** What already exists in supplier, cold chain, finance, retail and food access? Which connections are unnecessary or harmful?
4. **Costs and risks:** What property, operating, staffing, food-safety, insurance and capital thresholds need actual estimates?
5. **Pilot:** Is there an approved smallest meaningful experiment, and what counts as success or failure?
6. **Return:** Who receives the findings, can correct them, and can insist that public summaries be withheld?

**The heap doesn't get to answer those questions for them.**
