# RIGHT HANDS × KINSHIP × ABUNDENT
## The Living Neighbor Network — One card system. Many doors. One verified return.
**Grand planZ shard · 2026-10-21 · HEAP-CUT-001 / FIELD-001 / COMMON-TABLE-WASECA-001**

**Disposition:** PROPOSED — HOLD FOR INDEPENDENT FIELD AUTHORIZATION  
**Human design decision:** Treat the entire emerging regional grocery / mutual-capacity network as the one complete thing that crosses planZ. Connect a locally stewarded, changing card system to optional, producer-controlled radio broadcasts and a familiar portal inside a station's website or listening app. A heard need should remain actionable after the air moment; a volunteered response should become a relationship with the correct local steward; real confirmation should change the next available card snapshot.

> **THE CARD IS THE ADDRESS. THE RADIO IS THE VOICE. THE PORTAL IS THE DOOR. THE STEWARD IS THE HAND. THE RETURN IS THE WITNESS.**
>
> One card network. Many independent doors. Nobody has to give up their authority to participate.

This is the **integrating master shard** of the October 21 concept. It binds—not replaces—[HEAP-CUT-001](../heap-cut/HEAP-CUT-001-WHOLE-GROCERY-NETWORK.md), [KINSHIP-BROADCAST-QUEUE-001](../heap-cut/KINSHIP-BROADCAST-QUEUE-001.md), [KINSHIP-MORNING-BOARD-001](../heap-cut/KINSHIP-MORNING-BOARD-001.md), and [KINSHIP-PORTAL-001](../heap-cut/KINSHIP-PORTAL-001.md). The web/app portal and broadcast tools in this branch are **fictional offline prototypes**, not a deployed service or approved station integration. **Kinship Radio**, the station under discussion, and its app vendor have **not** participated in or authorized this build. The proposed grocery also has not adopted it.

## I. What crosses

**Not a building. Not one grocery delivery. Not a proprietary marketplace. Not the station.**

The object crossing is **one source-linked, consent-governed network of physical needs and offers, independent participants and changing outcomes**:

- People and local organizations can *propose* a need, offer, facility, resource, event or possible match.
- The appropriate steward verifies its own limited claim and independently authorizes the specific public card.
- A public card retains a stable address and versioned, expiring *current state*. A changed/withdrawn card is not silently erased from historical receipts.
- A radio producer may choose a **dated shortlist** for broadcast; this is optional, separate permission. No station controls source cards or participant relationships.
- A listener can hear a card, later visit a familiar website or app portal, inspect its **current** status and optionally express an offer.
- The offer is routed **to the responsible source steward**; the steward may coordinate, reject, pause or invite follow-up. It never becomes a pre-assigned task.
- Any actual fulfillment is reported by the operator and independently confirmed or disputed by the recipient; then the steward changes the source card.
- A fresh reviewed radio edition / public page represents what is now open, not what a past recording once claimed.
- planZ records what was proposed, who had authority, source evolution and the admissible return; it does not govern participants.

## II. What the user actually experiences

**The radio encounter**

A human morning-show host, if station leadership opts in, chooses a current 20-, 45- or 90-second segment from a verified candidate queue. The host might say that a community organization has an open food-sorting opportunity and invite listeners to check its card. The script should be useful even if the host has only twenty seconds of airtime, or if no currently suitable card is available.

**The portal encounter**

A person opens a hypothetical **Neighbors / Right Hands** button in Kinship's website or listening app. The **same public, steward-approved webpage** shows recent local opportunities with large actions, coarse area, expiry, clearly displayed open/paused/filled/withdrawn status, and a plain safe-response door. No account or church membership should be required to *browse*. Any real submission would need a separately approved privacy and abuse-prevention design.

**The local encounter**

A listener taps **I might help**. A local steward—not a radio automation—receives the interest and decides whether coordination is appropriate. The card can PAUSE when capacity has been reached. If a task actually completes, the steward reports and an appropriate independent actor confirms or disputes. The old radio announcement stays an honest historical snapshot; the public webpage now says **FILLED**.

**The return**

A future producer might read a new card, not mention an already closed one, or (with separately granted story/broadcast rights) tell an outcome story. planZ updates a source-linked network passport. **People don't need to know planZ to benefit from it.**

| Organ | Project / owner possibility | Job | What it does NOT own |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **1. Physical food/aid economy** | Local producers, prospective grocery, independent aid organizations | Real inventory, safety, sale, distribution and recipient needs | Radio editorial choices or public-card technical authority |
| **2. Source stewardship** | Actual local organizers, responsible helper, qualified institution | Verify need/offer, public consent, safe reply contact, fulfillment status | Permission to require anyone to join Kinship or a church |
| **3. Card protocol** | RIGHT HANDS / POSTYMON / REALITY.2 candidate forms | Stable ID, public-safe summary, revision, expiry, refusal, typed need/offer | Identity verification by cryptographic signature alone |
| **4. Abundent public return** | FIELD-001 / webZ proposal | One user-facing card state/address and printable/QR-compatible public door | Ownership of organizations, real people, private source records |
| **5. Radio editorial** | Kinship (independent station; *only if invited*) | Producer review, language, slot, schedule, decision to air or refuse | Co-op business, beneficiary verification, volunteer assignment |
| **6. Station web/app carrier** | Kinship website + actual authorized app operator/vendor | Optional button, link or Web View to the owner-approved public board | A card's current truth, privacy defaults, automatic app features |
| **7. Human response & witness** | Responding person, appropriate steward and recipient | Offer → acceptance → report → independent confirmation/dispute | Proof of aid merely because someone clicked or heard an announcement |
| **8. planZ heap** | planZ / HEAP-CUT-001 | Preserve source ancestry, possible compositions, stage gates and returned receipts | The authority to merge all organizations or declare partnerships |

GHoT/reLATTE/Jubilee/PENNY may contribute **candidate instruments** for provenance, resilient handoffs, voluntary help and receipt models. This proposal imports none of their runtime claims into a live deployment. PENNY help is not grocery cash, exchangeable credit or co-op equity.

## IV. Two coupled but separately authorized clocks

**Card clock:** A source owns an editable, versioned current state, with independent `OPEN → PAUSED / FILLED / WITHDRAWN / EXPIRED` and immutable earlier revisions. Every card has a last-confirmed time and a deadline. Unverified or stale public needs stop being presented as current.

**Broadcast clock:** An editor owns a dated, independent `CANDIDATE → HOLD/REFUSE/APPROVE → SCHEDULED → CONFIRMED_AIRED` chain. **A radio show cannot be an instant truth feed**, and a drafted edition can become stale before air. Pre-air recheck is required, and a universal no-specific-need fallback must be possible. Only an actual station-controlled human witness can confirm an air event.

The web/app portal follows the *current card clock*. Broadcast history follows the *broadcast clock*. Neither overwrites the other.

**Real input is not a public consequence:** `LISTENER_OFFER != ACCEPTED_HELP`, `FULFILLMENT_REPORT != RECIPIENT_CONFIRMATION`, `STATION_APPROVAL != AUTO_AIR`.

## V. The first audience isn't an app user—it's a human producer

Start from a **practical station value**: short, safe, regionally relevant, human-editable, source-checked material for a morning show. Producers are free to use a single card, ask one engagement question, skip the whole segment, or reject every candidate. We must not infer from a conversational observation that the station or hosts want this exact product.

The proposed Morning Board has distinct doors for **ONE CARD**, **NEIGHBOR MOMENT**, **COMMUNITY ROUNDUP**, **QUESTION**, and **VERIFIED RETURN**. This is editorial *preparation*, not an automated radio host or new live ad inventory.

The app/website portal can be introduced in parallel as one optional way listeners respond. It is *not* necessary to modify Kinship's existing app to test an offline idea: first prove that the current-state webpage makes sense to a participant. Any future use of an app tab/Web View, link handling, or continued audio playback must be verified with Kinship and the actual vendor on real devices.

### Proposed conversation, if invited

> "What if your morning show had an optional, freshly checked community-needs board—short enough for a twenty-second mention? Listeners could open a 'Neighbors' link on your website or, if your app allows it, inside your listening app. Actual local organizations would keep needs current and respond to offers; your producers retain full editorial control. We've only built a fictional demonstration, and we'd value your feedback on whether it would be useful."

Do **not** contact the station or represent Kinship as a partner without an authorized human decision to do so.

## VI. Modes for the same public card

| Door | Strength | Constraint |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Physical print/QR** | Works at gatherings, co-op counter, bulletin boards, listening parties | QR must link to safe, current public state; paper may be stale |
| **Abundent or approved independent website** | Source-approved canonical/compatible card lookup and relationship continuation | Proposed only; no real intake or host claim |
| **Kinship on-air mention** | Radio makes possibilities audible across an existing audience | Editorial consent, dated snapshot, never automatic |
| **Kinship station website** | Listener can see cards while following a show | Station admin must opt in; external-source labels remain clear |
| **Kinship listening-app link/Web View** | Possible friction-reduced response while listening | Vendor/station capability and streaming behavior unverified |
| **Other consenting nodes** | Other churches, community radio, cafes or libraries could choose to carry public cards | Every local owner may decline; no blanket federation or rollout |

An organization can carry one door and not another. No exclusive platform, forced data sharing, paid participation or automatic station adoption.

The smallest credible later live card requires:

```text
publicCardId                     stable, unguessing opaque public identifier
revision + owner-source pointer  exact source lineage
sourceSteward                    accountable human/organization (not public identity leak)
category + safe abstract         human-reviewed material need/offer/event
coarseRegion                     no beneficiary pickup/delivery address
sourceState                      OPEN / PAUSED / FILLED / WITHDRAWN / EXPIRED
checkedAt + expiresAt            freshness and sunset
boardPublicationConsent          scope, owner decision, revocation
radioPublicationConsent          separate decision, not inferred from board
stationEditorialReview           independent Kinship act (if Kinship opts in)
safeResponseRoute                steward-owned, private by design, tested/maintained
fulfillmentReceiptRefs           private/source-scoped proof; not self-report == witness
```

No live contact collection exists in the current HTML. A future form must provide explicit consent, rate limits, moderation, operator ownership and a safe reply path. The online portal must never display private source records, public helper rankings, track listener profiles, redirect charitable money into commercial operations, or force religious participation.

## VIII. A crossing that can fail and still count

**Demo A — one useful loop:** fictional open transport card appears in current website board → a Kinship producer could hypothetically select a dated item → a listener expresses a simulated offer → steward pauses → simulated independent confirmation closes the card → next mock producer edition omits it; old audio would still point to current-state address.

**Demo B — failure/repair:** card was withdrawn between producer selection and air → station candidate becomes unsafe; no item announced as open; public portal shows withdrawn, not an empty or invented replacement.

**Demo C — radio refuses:** Kinship chooses not to participate → grocery/source stewards and Abundent-or-paper cards remain coherent; station does not become a missing essential organ.

**Demo D — app unsupported:** vendor does not support a custom tab or audio continuity fails → an ordinary HTTPS link or non-app browser still works, after actual source approval; no claim the app integrates.

**Demo E — not a story:** recipient confirms a completed food handoff but declines publicity → card closes, public outcome is minimal, no identifiable on-air testimonial.

This is a *complete-system modeling test*, not a live end-to-end pilot.

## X. The system's success criterion

Not number of page views. Not maximum airtime. Not total cards created. Not AI output volume.

**Can someone hear a source-approved local opportunity, find its true current status through an appropriate doorway, offer help without coercion or exposing a vulnerable person, and receive a legitimate human return?**

And can that remain true if a card is withdrawn, the station declines, the app does not support the portal, or a delivery fails?

When that works under real independently consenting owners, *one physical relationship crosses the network*. When repeatable under multiple independent actors, the complete network begins to have field evidence. We must never promote prototype UI to these statuses.
