## New inference (not an inherited project claim)

These sources expose a seam between four independently real subjects:

1. **Carrier:** the particular physical expression and its encoding/edition.
2. **Observation:** what the instrument actually measured and could/could not decode.
3. **Encounter:** declared score + observer + uncertainty + actual local choice.
4. **Consequence:** separately authorized local effect with a new receipt.

The new hypothesis is **FIELD-SCORE-001**: bind these without pretending that a carrier claims physical verification, that instrument reception grants consent, that a rendering rewrites the world, or that multiple copied signals are independent witnesses.

This inference is ours on this planZ branch. It is **not** a claim that any donor repo already implemented it, approved it, or selected it into canon.
